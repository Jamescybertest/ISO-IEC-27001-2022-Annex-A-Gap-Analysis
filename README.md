# ISO-IEC-27001-2022-Annex-A-Gap-Analysis


Organization: MapleTrust Financial Services Inc. (Fictional)  
Assessment: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Gap Analysis  
Assessment Type: Internal Information security Control Gap Assessment  
Status: In Progress

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the application of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 to Conduct an ISO 27001 Annex A gap assessment for MapleTrust Financial Services, a fictional Canadian financial institution with 250 employees

The objective is to evaluate existing information security controls, identify gaps against applicable requirements, and recommend corrective actions based on associated security risks.

## 1. Assessment Scope

The assessment covers information security controls supporting MapleTrust's financial services operations, including: Customer information and financial records, Employee access to information systems, Information security policies and procedures, security awareness and training.

## 2. Assessment Criteria

Controls are evaluated across the four Annex A themes:

1.  A.5 Organizational Controls  
2.  A.6 People Controls  
3.  A.7 Physical Controls  
4.  A.8 Technological Controls

## Assessment Methodology

1. **Control Review:**  Identify applicable Annex A controls and relevant internal policy requirements.  
2. **Evidence Evaluation:** Review simulated policies, procedures, and operational records to establish the current implementation status.  
3. **Gap Identification:** Compare existing controls against assessment criteria and document deficiencies.
4. **Risk Assessment:** Evaluate identified gaps based on likelihood and potential business impact.
5. **Corrective Actions:** Recommend remediation measures and track their implementation.

### **Implementation Ratings:** Implemented | Partially Implemented | Not Implemented | Pending Verification | Not Applicable


| Annex A Control | Status | Identified Gap | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.5.1 — Policies for Information Security | Partially Implemented (Preliminary) | Security policy not reviewed for four years; no assigned policy owner | Assign policy ownership and establish periodic reviews |
| A.6.3 — Information Security Awareness, Education and Training | Partially Implemented | Only 60% of employees completed mandatory annual training | Establish centralized completion tracking and formal follow up procedures |

## Risk Assessment

Risk Scoring Methodology
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact, Both likelihood and impact are rated on a scale of 1–5.  
Risk Rating Scale:
Low: 1–4
Medium: 5–9
High: 10–15
Critical: 16–25

| Control | Identified Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Score | Risk Level | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A.5.1 – Security Policies | Outdated policies may lead to inconsistent security practices. | Medium (3) | High (4) | **12/25** | High | Assign policy owner and establish regular reviews. |
| A.6.3 – Security Awareness | Employees may be more vulnerable to phishing attacks. | High (4) | High (4) | **16/25** | Critical | Implement training tracking and follow-up procedures. |
